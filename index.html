METHODOLOGY & DISCLAIMER

This is an educational demonstration, not investment advice. Prices are end-of-day closes pulled once per day and are not live or intraday. Do not trade on anything shown here.

Growth is measured over the selected horizon and every series is indexed to 100 at the start of that window. The 30 and 90 day views use daily closes; the 1 and 5 year views use weekly closes. Closes are adjusted for stock splits - without that, any name that has split reads as a catastrophic loss over long horizons. Names listed for less than the full window are indexed from their first available session, so their line starts partway across and longer horizons read n/a. Category membership is editorial: each name sits in the layer of the AI stack where the bulk of its AI revenue sits, and appears once only.

REFRESH: Once daily at 00:00 Singapore time (16:00 UTC). Every figure shown is derived from the price series alone. No fundamentals, no estimates.
